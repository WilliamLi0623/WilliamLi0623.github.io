Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Waterloo · Exchange student at National Taiwan University · Networking & Systems

I’m an Honours Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Waterloo, currently spending Fall 2026 on exchange at National Taiwan University. At NTU I take graduate courses in advanced computer networks (ns-3 simulation labs) and network virtualization (OpenFlow/P4 labs for programmable data planes).

Research interests. I study networked systems, especially:

  • Programmable networks: SDN, P4, and programmable data planes
  • Transport protocols and congestion control
  • Datacenter networking: RDMA (RoCEv2, InfiniBand) and PFC/ECN
  • Network performance analysis and measurement

I come to networking from running real systems. As an SRE co-op at TextNow, I built Datadog monitoring for IPv6 neighbor-table exhaustion that caught a previously unmonitored DNS failure mode, and I right-sized the CI fleet for a projected ~33% cost saving. On the UW Computer Science Club Terminal Committee I help run the club’s Linux servers, core services, and public mirror. At home I keep an RDMA networking testbed and a Go control plane for Proxmox and K3s. I also hold the Juniper JNCIS-SP certification.

I’m looking for research opportunities in networking and systems: undergraduate research or RA positions, summer research internships, and research-oriented co-op terms. I plan to apply to graduate school. If you work on related problems, please email me.
